Chess Table: Help

Write to kccoder@gmail.com with any question. We usually answer within a few days.

The coach stopped a move. Why?

When a move would lose something, the coach holds it and asks a question. Tap "Take it back" to try again, "Show me" to see why, or "Play it anyway" to let the move stand.

How do I turn the sound and the voice off?

Use the speaker button in a game, "Sound on" on the home screen, or "Sound and voice" in the grown-up corner. One switch silences the sound effects and every spoken line.

How do I add or rename a player?

Grown-ups, then "Who is playing". Each player can be a young player or a grown-up, and can move pieces by dragging or only by tapping.

A piece lands on a square I didn't mean

Open the Touch lab from the grown-up corner, try twenty moves with each way of dragging, and use the one that hits the star most often for that player.

The screen says it got stuck

Tap "Try again" or "Back home". Your games, players and stars are saved. If it happens again, write to us and tell us what you were doing.

Moving to a new iPhone or iPad

Chess Table keeps everything on the device, so it moves with your device backup when you set up the new one from that backup. You can also tap "Save a backup" in the grown-up corner and keep the file in Files or send it to yourself, then on the new device tap "Restore from a backup file" and choose it.

Where is the grown-up corner?

At the bottom of the home screen, "Grown-ups". It asks a short sum written in words before it opens, so children stay out of settings.
