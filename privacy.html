Chess Table: Privacy

Last updated November 18, 2026.

Chess Table collects nothing

There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send anything over the internet.

What stays on this device

Players' first names and colors, games and puzzles, stars and Good Sport points, settings, and touch lab results. They are kept inside the app on this iPhone or iPad, and in your own device backup if backups are on. We never see them.

Sharing a game or a backup

After answering the grown-up question, a grown-up can share a finished game as text from Look back, or save a backup of players, games and stars as a file from the grown-up corner. Nothing is shared unless a grown-up chooses to.

Children

Chess Table is made for children playing with their families. Chat is picked from ready-made, kind phrases; nobody can type a message, and there is no way to talk to people outside the device. The coach speaks with the voice built into iOS, on the device.

Erasing everything

"Erase everything" in the grown-up corner removes every player, game, puzzle, star and setting from this device. Deleting the app does the same.

Feedback you choose to send

If you send feedback by email or through Apple's TestFlight, we see only what you send, and use it only to answer you and improve the app.
